# Cultural and Originality Guide

BattleRaja uses a fictional world inspired by the Indian subcontinent.

## Use

- Fictional forts, courtyards, stepwells, bazaars, ports, mangroves and monsoon environments
- Textile and craft motifs as considered accents
- Broad regional research
- Original character silhouettes and names
- Cultural source/provenance notes

## Avoid

- Combat in recognisable active sacred sites
- Gods or revered figures as ordinary combat characters
- Caste, religion, ethnicity or nationality as stats
- Accent-based humour or community caricature
- Politically contested maps/symbols without expert review
- Copying reference-game characters, maps, UI, sound or effects

Public release requires cultural and naming review.
