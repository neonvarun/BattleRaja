# Milestone 5 Report — Complete Offline Battle Royale

## Gate status

**Provisional technical pass.** The MovementLab now starts a deterministic eight-actor
offline match with explicit phases, Aandhi zone data, outside-zone damage through the
central pipeline, health pickups, elimination/placement/winner state, spectator camera
handoff and rematch reload. Twenty accelerated pure matches complete. A full five-minute
physical playthrough, pacing/readability, results UX and repeated-runtime memory review
remain open.

## Implemented

- Pure `OfflineMatchDefinition`/`OfflineMatchSimulation` with 298-second Solo Raja
  phases: warm-up, spawn protection, opening, pressure and final circle.
- Data-driven Aandhi center/radius/outside-damage values, separated spawn validation,
  participant health/position snapshots, idempotent elimination, stable placement and
  winner state.
- `OfflineMatchController` bridge for the existing player plus seven bots; outside-zone
  damage is sent through `CombatDamageResolver` as `DamageType.Aandhi`.
- Three respawning neutral health pickups (`MatchPickup`).
- Elimination disables player pointer/input flow and redirects the camera to a living
  spectator target; TAB cycles targets. Results overlay and R scene reload provide the
  local rematch path.
- `OfflineMatchHud` shows phase, alive count, zone radius, spectator and results state.
- No gadgets, Pehel/Maya, accounts, progression, monetisation, Photon, PlayFab or
  production release systems were added.
