# Milestone 6 Report — Jugaad Gadgets

## Gate status

**Provisional technical pass.** The offline lab now has three data-driven gadgets with
one-slot inventory, pickup/use validation, contextual bot use, bounded effects and HUD/
touch feedback. Human balance, readability and low-end performance review remain open.

## Implemented

- Pure `GadgetDefinition`, `GadgetCatalog`, `GadgetInventory`, `GadgetRuntime`,
  `GadgetUseCommand` and `GadgetSpawnRules` contracts in the Domain assembly.
- Stable Gadget content IDs and serialized M6 definition assets for Umbrella Guard,
  Dhol Burst and Tiffin Station.
- Umbrella Guard directional facing state with short-duration projectile mitigation;
  Aandhi and generic damage remain counters that bypass the guard.
- Dhol Burst radial bounded displacement through the existing CharacterController path;
  it does not stun-lock or mutate health directly.
- Tiffin Station validated placement, finite lifetime, destroyable health and capped
  periodic healing through `CombatHealth`.
- Gadget pickups, one-slot inventory, keyboard `G`, touch button, feedback HUD and
  bot context evaluation (retreat/low-health pressure rather than random firing).
- MovementLab now spawns one pickup of each gadget and serializes the M6 user/HUD
  references for the player and seven bots.
- Damage requests carry projectile hit direction so shield mitigation can respect the
  explicit facing arc. No Photon, PlayFab, progression, monetisation or final art was
  added.

## Performance observations

- Gadget definitions and inventory are bounded value/state objects; no gadget effect
  adds a per-frame managed collection in the normal player path.
- Tiffin healing currently scans scene health components once per heal interval and is
  suitable for the eight-actor lab, but should be replaced by a registered target set
  before larger matches.
- M6 development artifacts are approximately 83.4 MB APK and 113.5 MB Web output.
- No formal Editor Profiler, Lava/Oppo CPU/GPU/GC capture or browser memory/frame-time
  capture was collected.

## Warnings, failures and limitations

- Grey-box geometry, immediate-mode HUD and primitive gadget telegraphs are placeholders.
- Umbrella arc, Dhol displacement feel, Tiffin interruption rules and bot use rates need
  human balance review; no claim of human balance validation is made.
- Gadget pickups do not respawn in this first vertical slice; match restart reloads the
  scene and restores them.
- Runtime station destruction is exposed through its central `CombatHealth` target;
  visual damage feedback and authoring of arena-safe placement remain minimal.
- External services, signing, hosting, store submission, legal/privacy, cultural review
  and final branding remain outside M6.
