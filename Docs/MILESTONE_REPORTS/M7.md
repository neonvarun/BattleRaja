# Milestone 7 Report — Three-Fighter Vertical Slice

## Gate status

**Provisional technical pass with explicit alpha-slice debt.** Bijli, Pehel and Maya
now have stable data-driven definitions and are present as distinct actors in the
offline lab. Pehel/Maya use distinct health, movement, basic-attack and ability tuning.
The full art/audio/tutorial/menu/accessibility polish described by the prompt remains
replaceable alpha work and is recorded as human/production debt.

## Implemented

- `FighterDefinition.Pehel` and `.Maya` with stable fighter/ability IDs and distinct
  health, movement and basic-attack tuning.
- Pure `FighterSpecialDefinition` contracts for Pehel Charge Throw and Maya Decoy plus
  bounded `DecoyRuntime` movement/expiry rules.
- Serialized `M7-Pehel.asset`, `M7-Maya.asset`, `M7-PehelSweep.asset` and
  `M7-MayaShard.asset` content definitions.
- MovementLab generation now serializes two Bijli, three Pehel and two Maya bots while
  retaining the player Bijli, offline match, gadget pickups and M5 spectator/results flow.
- Existing shared movement/attack/ability command paths remain in use; no fighter gets a
  direct Transform/health authority path.
- No Photon, PlayFab, purchases, public deployment or final branded/protected assets were
  added.
