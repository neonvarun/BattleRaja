# BattleRaja V1.0 Play Content-Rating Preparation — Draft

This is a factual preparation sheet for the owner completing Google Play's official
target-audience and content-rating questionnaires. It is not an assigned rating.

Official reference: `https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898843`.

## Current V1 facts to verify against the final signed build

- Category: game; stylized top-down 3D battle royale.
- Gameplay: one local human fights seven fictional bots in short matches.
- Combat: fictional characters use projectiles, dashes, charges, throws, knockback,
  gadgets and a closing storm zone; defeated characters are eliminated from the match.
- Presentation: colourful stylized toy-box visuals; no realistic injury detail, gore,
  blood, sexual content, drugs or political/religious representation is intended.
- User-generated content: none.
- Social features: none.
- Ads and purchases: none in V1 scope.
- Gambling or prize mechanics: none.
- Account, location and network access: none intended for the offline candidate.

## Owner review gates

1. Confirm the exact signed AAB still matches these facts and its bundled SDK inventory.
2. Select the target audience in Play Console; do not infer an age rating from this draft.
3. Complete the official IARC/content-rating questionnaire accurately.
4. Obtain legal and cultural review of the fighter names, copy, screenshots and artwork.
5. Record the resulting ratings and any required regional disclosures before submission.
