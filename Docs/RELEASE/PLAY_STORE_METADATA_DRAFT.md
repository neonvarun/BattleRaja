# BattleRaja Play Store metadata draft

This is working copy for owner and legal review. It is not a submission and must not be
treated as an approved claim.

## Short description draft

Drop into a colourful offline micro battle royale. Pick a Raja, outplay seven bots and
survive the Aandhi.

## Full description draft

BattleRaja is an original, offline-first top-down 3D micro battle royale built for quick,
readable matches. Enter Bazaar Bastion with one of three fictional fighters, collect a
tactical gadget, read the closing Aandhi and outlast seven bots.

Choose your style:

- Bijli: dash through danger and punish bad positioning.
- Pehel: charge, capture and throw with clear counterplay windows.
- Maya: place a decoy and create a deceptive escape route.

Every match is local. No account, matchmaking or internet connection is required for the
offline mode. Finish the tutorial, spectate after elimination, inspect your placement and
rematch immediately.

## Feature claims that require evidence before publication

- Offline play works in airplane mode on the supported Android test matrix.
- All three fighters and all three gadgets are readable and functional on the final APK.
- The tutorial teaches movement, aim, attack, ability, gadget, Aandhi, elimination and
  results in order.
- Settings include left-handed controls, aim assist, reduced flashes, high contrast, text
  scale and audio controls.

## Data safety draft (not approved)

The intended V1.0 offline candidate collects no account, location, contacts, messages or
payment data and does not require a network connection. This statement must be rechecked
against the final Unity player, Android manifest, analytics configuration and any third-party
SDK before the owner submits a Data safety form.
