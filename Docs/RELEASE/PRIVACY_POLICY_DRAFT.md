# BattleRaja V1.0 Privacy Policy — Draft

**Draft status:** owner and legal review required. This document is not a legal notice,
does not identify the final publisher, and must not be published unchanged.

## Scope

This draft describes the planned BattleRaja V1.0 offline Android build. V1.0 is a local
single-player game with one human player and seven bots. It does not include accounts,
multiplayer, matchmaking, cloud progression, ads, purchases or online leaderboards.

## Information handled

The V1.0 app is designed not to collect or transmit personal information. It stores a
small amount of game preference data locally on the device, such as accessibility/audio
settings, selected fighter and tutorial-completion state. This local data is not linked to
an account and is not uploaded by the offline build.

The app does not intentionally request contacts, location, messages, photos, microphone,
camera or payment information. The current offline candidate does not require network
access and does not include an analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or cloud-backend
service. The final signed artifact and bundled SDK inventory must be checked before this
statement is approved.

## Sharing and retention

No V1.0 user data is intended to be shared with the publisher or third parties. Local
preferences remain on the device until the user changes them, clears app data or
uninstalls the app. If a tester voluntarily sends a screenshot or diagnostic log to the
support contact, that material is handled only for support and testing under the owner's
approved retention policy; users must remove private information before sending it.

## Children and ratings

The owner must choose the target-audience declaration and complete the official Google
Play content-rating questionnaire using the exact final build. This draft does not make a
children's-privacy or age-rating determination.
