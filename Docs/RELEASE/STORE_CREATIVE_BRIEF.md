# BattleRaja V1.0 store creative brief

## Visual direction

Colourful toy-box arena, warm terracotta and teal, saffron signal accents, strong fighter
silhouettes and readable action rings. The art must feel like an original fictional bazaar
world, not a reproduction of a reference game's character, arena, logo, sound or UI.

## Required captures

1. App icon: a simple original BattleRaja mark that remains legible at launcher size.
2. Feature graphic: the three fictional fighters around the Bazaar Bastion landmark with a
   clear offline-first message.
3. Phone captures: menu, fighter selection, match opening, active Aandhi, gadget use,
   spectator/results and settings on the final Lava APK.
4. Tablet/large-screen captures only after the supported aspect-ratio policy is approved.

## Review checklist

- No placeholder/debug labels, Unity splash branding or generated test names.
- No colour-only combat state; every screenshot retains shape, text or motion cues.
- Touch targets and safe-area margins are visible at the supported portrait size.
- No paid/external asset appears without a recorded licence and owner approval.
- Cultural and legal review signs off before any public upload.
