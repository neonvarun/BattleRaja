## Short description

BattleRaja is a stylised top-down micro battle royale with fast matches, readable combat,
original subcontinent-inspired fiction, Jugaad Gadgets and a closing Aandhi zone.

## Feature bullets

- Pick a distinct fighter and learn a compact arena.
- Combine movement, projectile attacks and temporary gadgets.
- Survive the Aandhi and outplay seven offline opponents in the current lab.
- Android and desktop Web are the intended platforms; online services are not enabled in this
  candidate.
